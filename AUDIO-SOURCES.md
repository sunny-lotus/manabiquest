# Recorded audio sources

Library: VSCO-2-CE by Versilian Studios and contributing performers.

License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE

Processing: leading silence trimmed, short excerpts, stereo downmixed to mono, low-pass filtered and resampled to 22,050 Hz, peak normalized, faded at the boundaries, and exported as 16-bit PCM WAV.

The source library labels middle C as C3; pitched playback roots use scientific pitch notation (middle C = MIDI 60). The score and runtime arrangement are original to the project.

## Battle-result songs

- `common/audio/result-defeat-v1.mp3`: the saved original project Nocturne piano recording (30.5 seconds). Encoded from the preserved WAV without changing its notes or arrangement. Piano sample credits remain in the in-game recording credits.
- `common/audio/result-victory-v1.mp3`: original four-bar C-major fanfare composed for this project. Recorded brass, strings, flute and timpani: VSCO 2 Community Edition; glockenspiel: VCSL. Both libraries are CC0-1.0, by Versilian Studios and contributing performers.
- `common/audio/victory-score.json`: the original victory arrangement, tempo 116 BPM. Offline sample rendering with gentle echoes and fades; exported as mono 22.05 kHz / 80 kbps MP3.
